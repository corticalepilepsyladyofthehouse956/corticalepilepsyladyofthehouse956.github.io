😕 Ever Wonder Why Your AI Ignored a Skill?

You've carefully crafted your SKILL.md files. You've given your AI agent clear instructions. But sometimes, it just doesn't use them. Why? Because two or more skills have routing collisions — they're too similar, and the AI can't tell which one to use. It's frustrating, confusing, and often invisible.

skillsonar fixes that. It's a straightforward tool that looks at every SKILL.md file in a folder, compares them using a search-engine-like mathematical method (called BM25), and tells you exactly which skills are blocking each other. No internet required. No cloud. It runs completely on your machine — deterministic, repeatable, offline.

🔧 Technical Notes (Skip If You Want)

Under the hood, skillsonar uses a pipeline of text normalization, tokenization, and BM25 distance scoring. It reads every SKILL.md file, extracts the frontmatter title and the first 200 words of the body, then builds a scoring matrix. The output is a ranked list of file pairs with a collision score from 0 to 100. A score above 70 means "high risk". The tool also suggests a safe rename — typically adding a distinguishing word to one of the conflicting skills.
